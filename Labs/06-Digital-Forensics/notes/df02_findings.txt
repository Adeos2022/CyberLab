Evidence:
Scripts/sample.log

Analysis tool:
Scripts/log_parser.py

Findings:

1. Four failed SSH authentication attempts were identified.
2. The username "root" was targeted three times.
3. The username "guest" was targeted once.
4. Source IP 192.168.1.50 generated three failed attempts.
5. Source IP 10.0.0.5 generated one failed attempt.
6. Two successful "admin" authentication events were present in the
   sample log but were correctly excluded from the failed-login counts.

Primary observation:
192.168.1.50 generated the highest number of failed authentication
attempts and repeatedly targeted the root account.

Assessment:
The repeated failures are suspicious within this controlled laboratory
dataset, but the evidence alone does not establish malicious intent.

Conclusion:
