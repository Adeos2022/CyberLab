DF-06 — Evidence Correlation & Incident Timeline

Case:
Controlled CyberLab forensic investigation

Evidence directory:
Labs/06-Digital-Forensics/evidence/df06_case/

Baseline:
Labs/06-Digital-Forensics/evidence/df06_baseline_hashes.txt
Labs/06-Digital-Forensics/evidence/df06_baseline_timeline.txt

Analysis tools:
sha256sum
find
Scripts/log_parser.py
Scripts/strings_extractor.py

Authentication findings:

1. Three failed SSH authentication attempts were identified.
2. The username "admin" was targeted twice.
3. The username "root" was targeted once.
4. Source IP 10.10.10.25 generated all three failed attempts.
5. A successful authentication for analyst from 10.10.10.10
   was present and was correctly excluded from failed-login counts.

File-system correlation:

1. auth.log remained unchanged from the established baseline.
2. system.conf remained unchanged from the established baseline.
3. notes.txt was modified after the baseline was established.
4. downloaded_file.txt was modified after the baseline was established.
5. suspicious_script.sh was not present in the baseline and was
   identified as a newly created artifact.

Timeline correlation:

1. The original evidence baseline was established before the
   controlled incident activity.
2. notes.txt was subsequently modified.
3. suspicious_script.sh was subsequently created.
4. downloaded_file.txt was subsequently modified.
5. The resulting filesystem timestamps and SHA-256 comparisons
   distinguish unchanged, modified, and newly created artifacts.

Suspicious artifact analysis:

The newly created suspicious_script.sh contained a shell script
reference to:

http://example.com/update

The URL is an intentionally planted indicator in this controlled
laboratory scenario.

Assessment:

The evidence demonstrates a controlled sequence containing repeated
failed SSH authentication attempts followed by filesystem changes
and creation of a script containing a suspicious-looking URL.

The evidence establishes temporal and artifact correlation within the
laboratory dataset. It does not independently establish that the
source IP 10.10.10.25 caused the filesystem changes, nor does it
establish real-world malicious activity.

The SHA-256 baseline successfully distinguished modified and newly
created artifacts from unchanged files.

Conclusion:

DF-06 evidence correlation and incident timeline analysis PASSED.
