DF-07 — Evidence Acquisition, Preservation & Integrity Verification

Case:
CYBERLAB-DF07-2026-08-08

Evidence source:
Labs/06-Digital-Forensics/evidence/df07_case/original/

Acquisition:
Labs/06-Digital-Forensics/evidence/df07_case/acquisition/

Working copy:
Labs/06-Digital-Forensics/evidence/df07_case/working_copy/

Analysis:

Three controlled evidence files were established as the original
forensic evidence:

1. artifact.txt
2. incident_notes.txt
3. system_log.txt

The original files were hashed using SHA-256 before acquisition.

A file-level acquisition was then performed using cp -p. The
acquisition copy preserved the observed file timestamps, sizes, and
content.

SHA-256 verification confirmed that the acquisition hashes matched
the original evidence hashes.

A separate working copy was created from the verified acquisition.
The working-copy SHA-256 values also matched the original and
acquisition values.

File identification:

All three acquired artifacts were identified as ASCII text.

Content analysis:

artifact.txt contained a controlled laboratory evidence statement
and explicitly stated that no real malicious content was present.

incident_notes.txt documented the controlled forensic acquisition
exercise.

system_log.txt contained three controlled laboratory events:
service startup, an authentication event, and a monitoring event.

Integrity assessment:

Original, acquisition, and working-copy hashes matched for all three
evidence files.

The original evidence was preserved and subsequent analysis was
performed using the working copy.

Chain-of-custody assessment:

A chain-of-custody record was established documenting the case,
evidence source, acquisition location, acquisition method, hashing
method, integrity result, analyst, and acquisition date.

Conclusion:

DF-07 evidence acquisition, preservation, chain-of-custody handling,
and three-stage SHA-256 integrity verification PASSED.
